## 01 · 本田加注混动

**English**：Honda is putting three billion dollars into hybrids. It is also slowing down its electric car plans in the United States.

**中文**：本田将向混合动力投入三十亿美元，同时放慢在美国的电动车计划。

**讲解**：put money into 表示把钱投进某个方向；slow down 是放慢，比 stop 更准确，因为计划并没有取消。

**慢速句**：Honda is spending three billion dollars on hybrids. Its electric car plans are slowing down.

|---|---|---|
| hybrid | 混合动力 | a car that uses both fuel and electricity |
| put into | 投入 | to spend money or time on something |
| slow down | 放慢 | to become slower than before |

## 02 · 燃油机还有十年

**English**：Jaguar Land Rover says a new BYD SUV looks too much like its Defender. The dispute has led to checks at BYD's offices in the Netherlands.

| lead to | 导致 | to cause something to happen |

## 06 · 禾赛三季度增长

**English**：Hesai's revenue rose thirty-four percent last quarter. The lidar maker shipped sixty-five percent more units than a year earlier.

**中文**：禾赛上季度收入增长百分之三十四，这家激光雷达厂商的交付量比去年同期多百分之六十五。
